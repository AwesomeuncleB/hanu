How it works

Three steps, all on WhatsApp.

01

Save

Join through your cooperative or on your own. Contributions build a health balance that is locked for six months or one year.

02

Show your Hanu ID

At a partner clinic, show your Hanu ID for a consultation or a lab test. Nothing to pay at the desk if your balance covers it.

03

Hanu pays the clinic

We check the bill, pay the clinic directly at the negotiated price, and send you the receipt and your new balance.

The rules

Plain rules, written down.

Health money only works if it is still there on the day you are sick. So the rules are simple, and the same for everyone.

  • Locked for six months or one year

    You choose the term when you start. The balance is there for care throughout.

  • No cash withdrawals

    Hanu pays clinics, not people. That is what keeps the money for health.

  • If the bill is more than your balance

    Pay the difference yourself, or ask your community for voluntary support. Your group's own rules and approval apply.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Hanu insurance?

No. Hanu is a savings and payment platform. You are saving your own money for your own healthcare, and Hanu pays partner clinics from that balance.

Can I take my money out as cash?

No. Savings are locked for six months or one year and are paid only to clinics for care.

What does Hanu pay for?

Consultations and lab tests at partner clinics, usually at prices we have negotiated in advance.

What if my balance does not cover the bill?

You can pay the difference, or ask your community for voluntary support, subject to your group's rules and approval.

Who decides if a payment is refused?

A person, following written rules. Our software can flag a bill for review but it cannot deny one.
